AI-Powered Crop Health Dataset

Comprehensive dataset for training and evaluating AI models for early disease detection, precision nutrient optimization, and smart pest control in agriculture.

Dataset Overview

Image Data

High-resolution images of various crops, including healthy and diseased samples. Images cover different growth stages and environmental conditions.

  • Diverse crop types
  • Multiple disease categories
  • Varied lighting and background

Sensor Data

Data from various sensors, including soil moisture, temperature, humidity, and plant health metrics.

  • Soil composition data
  • Weather station data
  • GPS location data

Annotations

Detailed annotations for image and sensor data, including bounding boxes, segmentation masks, disease labels, nutrient levels, and pest information.

  • Precise bounding boxes
  • Semantic segmentation masks
  • Expert-verified labels

Dataset Features

Key Features

  • Large-scale, diverse dataset
  • Multi-modal data (image, sensor, annotations)
  • High-quality, expert-verified annotations
  • Suitable for various AI tasks
  • Regularly updated and expanded
